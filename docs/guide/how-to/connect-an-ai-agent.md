# Connect an AI agent

This guide shows you how to let an AI agent work in Cartograph for you.
An agent acts with your access and no more, and what it proposes waits
for you to merge.

You need **May use an agent** on your access (an administrator sets it on
the Access page). If **Agents** is missing from the rail, ask your
administrator to turn agents on.

## If your agent can open a browser

1. In the rail, choose **Agents**.
2. Under **Connect an agent**, press **Copy** beside the address.
3. Add that address to your agent's MCP settings.
4. When the agent connects, a page opens to sign you in and ask your
   permission. Allow it.

The agent appears in the list under **Connected**.

## If your agent cannot sign in through a browser

1. On the **Agents** page, open **A client that cannot sign in through the
   browser**.
2. Type a name under **Name the agent** and press **Create token**.
3. Press **Copy** at once. The token is shown only once and lasts 90 days
   unless you disconnect the agent.
4. Paste it into your agent's settings.
