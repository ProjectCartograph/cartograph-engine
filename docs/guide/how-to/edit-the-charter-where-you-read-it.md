# Edit the charter where you read it

This guide shows you how to change a project's charter on the charter
page itself. The example is the cooperative's *Quality Check Rollout*.

## Steps

1. Open the project's charter. See [Export the charter](export-the-charter.md),
   steps 1 to 3.
2. To change text, select it and type. An empty text part shows *Write
   here*. For the example, press **+ In scope** under **Scope**, and type
   *Grading every crate*.
3. To change a value that is chosen rather than typed, such as the
   programme or the outcomes served, select it. A panel opens beside it
   for that value (*Change programme*).
4. Some values are worked out from other records, such as the sponsor or
   the budget. Select one to change what it is worked out from (*Change
   what sponsor is worked out from*).
5. To add a row to a list, press its **+** button, for example **+ Key
   result**, **+ Problem** or **+ Out of scope**. To remove a row, press
   **×** at its end.

Your edits go into a change set named after the project, and the top bar
shows how many items are still to finish. Press **Merge** when you want
them in the workspace. See [Review and merge a change
set](review-and-merge-a-change-set.md).
