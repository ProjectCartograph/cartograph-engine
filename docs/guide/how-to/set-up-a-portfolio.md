# Set up a portfolio

This guide shows you how to define a portfolio: what it invests in, the strategic objectives it ranks against, who decides, and with which budgets. The example is the cooperative's *Member Services Portfolio*.

For why a portfolio exists and how it differs from a programme, see [About portfolio decisions](../explanation/about-portfolio-decisions.md). To fill it with work, see [Put a project or programme in a portfolio](put-a-project-or-programme-in-a-portfolio.md). For every field, see [Programme and portfolio fields](../reference/programme-and-portfolio-fields.md).

## Before you start

- The team that will decide must exist. The cooperative's *Member Services Team* does.
- The goals or objectives it ranks against must exist in Strategy. A portfolio can use goals and objectives only, not outcomes.
- If you want to name budgets or a review cycle, they must exist too. The cooperative has *Cooperative operating budget* and a *Seasonal Cycle*.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Portfolios**, then press **+ Portfolio**. The page *New portfolio* opens.
2. Type the **Name**, such as `Member Services Portfolio`.
3. In **Governed by**, choose the team that decides what to fund. Press **Create**.
   Cartograph opens the portfolio's first step and starts a change set named for the portfolio. Your work stays in it until you merge.
4. On **Aim**, write *What it invests in, and why* in a sentence. For example: `Fund first the member services that most help members sell what they grow.` The portfolio saves as you type.
5. Press **Next: strategy**. Under **Strategic objectives**, choose the goals or objectives the portfolio ranks work against. You can pick a suggestion, or search *Goal or area*. For the cooperative, choose *Grow member livelihoods*.
6. Press **Next: holds**. The page is headed **Contents**. It stays empty until projects and programmes say they belong. To fill it, see [Put a project or programme in a portfolio](put-a-project-or-programme-in-a-portfolio.md). Press **Next: governance**.
7. On **Governance**, add what applies:
   - **Budgets**: the budgets the portfolio hands out. Choose *Cooperative operating budget*.
   - **Review cycle**: how often it reviews its decisions. Choose *Seasonal Cycle*.
   - **Part of**: the larger portfolio this one sits inside, if any. Leave it empty for a top-level portfolio.
8. Press **Merge** at the top of the page, then **Merge** in the box that asks *Merge 1 record?*. The page shows *Merged into the workspace.*

## If something is not as you expect

- The **Checks** panel under each step lists what is missing. They advise, and do not stop you merging. The portfolio's three checks ask for a strategic objective, for something that names the portfolio, and for a decision on each thing in it.
- If you do not see the goal you want under **Strategic objectives**, it may be an outcome. Outcomes cannot be chosen. Pick the goal or objective above it.
- If you see *No goals or objectives yet. Define them in Goals first.*, add them in Goals, then come back.
- To change the review cycle or budgets later, open the portfolio and go to **Governance**.
