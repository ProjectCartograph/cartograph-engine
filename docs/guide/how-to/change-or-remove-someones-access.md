# Change or remove someone's access

This guide shows you how to change a person's roles and teams, or stop
them signing in. You need the **Administrator** role.

## Change it

1. In the rail, open **Access**. Find the person: the search box above
   the table finds a name or an address.
2. Press the icon at the end of their row, *Change access for <name>*.
3. Change their roles, teams or **May use an agent**, then press
   **Save**.

## Remove them

1. Press the bin icon at the end of their row, *Remove <name>*. You
   cannot remove yourself.
2. Read the box, *Remove <name>?*, and press **Remove**.

They can no longer sign in, unless their directory groups list them
again when they next sign in.
