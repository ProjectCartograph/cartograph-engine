# Write a programme's theory of change

This guide shows you how to write down how a programme's projects lead to its outcome: each step, what has to be true before it, why, and what it assumes. The example is the cooperative's *Market Access Programme*, which has no theory of change yet.

You need a programme that already exists, and the outcomes you want on its pathway should already be in Strategy. For why the programme needs one, see [About programmes, portfolios and projects](../explanation/about-programmes-portfolios-and-projects.md).

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Programmes** and select *Market Access Programme*. It opens on **Aim**.
2. Choose the step **Theory of change**. The page says *Nothing yet. Start with the outcome this programme is for.* The **Checks** panel says there is no theory of change yet.
3. Press **Outcome**. A card marked *Outcome* opens.
4. In **Outcome**, press *Choose an outcome* and pick the outcome the programme is for, for example *More produce is sold direct to buyers*.
5. In **Because**, write why the steps beneath it lead to this outcome, for example `Members who can reach retail buyers sell more of the season direct, instead of through the one buyer who sets the price.`
6. Press **Add what has to be true first**. A card marked *Precondition* opens beneath the first, indented.
7. In **Precondition**, pick the outcome that has to be true first, for example *Produce meets what retail buyers require*.
8. In **Because**, write why this has to happen first, and who makes it happen, for example `Retail buyers only take produce that passes their entry checks, so produce that fails them cannot be sold direct.`
9. Under **Assumptions**, press *Choose assumptions* and pick what has to be true for the step to work, for example *Buyers pay more for produce they can rely on*. If the assumption is not in the list, choose *Record an assumption* at the end of it.
10. Repeat from step 6 for each further precondition. To add one that rests on a precondition already on the pathway, use the line *Also needs something already on the pathway?* on the card that needs it.

Under a precondition that has nothing before it, the page says *Nothing on this pathway comes before Produce meets what retail buyers require, so the work in this programme produces it directly.* That line marks where the pathway starts, and where the programme's own projects do the work.

If a step rests on more than one precondition, the card says *All of these have to be true*, followed by the list. There is no "or". If two routes could both lead to an outcome, write two steps for it, each with its own reason and assumptions.

To read the pathway as a table, press **Results framework**. It lists each outcome with its reason and what produces it. Press **Back** to return.
