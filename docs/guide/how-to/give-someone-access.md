# Give someone access

This guide shows you how to let a person sign in to Cartograph and decide
what they can do. You need the **Administrator** role. The example adds a
colleague from the cooperative's quality team as a contributor.

Only people on the access list can sign in. A person who signs in without
being listed sees *You are not on the access list*.

## Steps

1. In the rail, open **Access**, under **Administration**.
2. Press **Add a person**.
3. Type their **Organisation address**. Their name is filled in from your
   directory when they first sign in.
4. Tick a role. Each role is listed with what it allows: **Reader**,
   **Contributor**, **Strategy editor** and **Administrator**. If you are
   unsure, tick **Reader** and raise it later. All four descriptions are
   also in [Kinds of record](../reference/kinds-of-record.md).
5. Tick their **Teams**. For the example, tick *Quality Team*. If their
   role allows it, they can edit the work of these teams and the teams
   under them.
6. **May use an agent** is ticked to begin with. Untick it if they should
   not use an AI agent. Their role must also allow agents.
7. Press **Save**.

Roles and teams that come from a directory group show ticked and cannot be
unticked here.

## If the person sees "You hold no role here"

They are listed, but none of their directory groups gives them a role.
Tick a role for them on the Access page.

## If there are no teams to tick

The box says *No teams yet. Add teams on the Teams sheet or through your
directory groups.* Open **Sheets**, then **Teams**, and add them.
