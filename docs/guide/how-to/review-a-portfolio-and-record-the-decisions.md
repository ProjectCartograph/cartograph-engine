# Review a portfolio and record the decisions

This guide shows you how to record a review: for each thing the portfolio holds, a decision, a priority and a reason. The example is the cooperative's *Cooperative Investment Portfolio*.

For what invest, hold and stop mean and why they are kept apart from the portfolio, see [About portfolio decisions](../explanation/about-portfolio-decisions.md).

## Before you start

The portfolio must hold at least one project, programme or portfolio. See [Put a project or programme in a portfolio](put-a-project-or-programme-in-a-portfolio.md).

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Portfolios** and open *Cooperative Investment Portfolio*. Press **Open**, then go to **Holds**. The page is headed **Contents**, and says *Decide for each project or programme: invest, hold or stop.*
2. Find each item. Items are grouped under *Programmes*, *Projects* and *Portfolios*.
3. For each item, press **Invest**, **Hold** or **Stop**.
   - **Invest** funds it and lets it go on.
   - **Hold** keeps it but commits nothing more until the next review.
   - **Stop** ends it and releases what it holds.
4. In the **Priority** box, type its rank. 1 is first. Two items may share a rank.
5. In the **Reason** box, say why in a sentence. For example: `A rejected delivery costs members the whole consignment, so quality moves earnings most this season.`
6. Press **Save decisions**. You see *Saved* beside the button.
7. Press **Merge** at the top of the page, then **Merge** in the box that opens.

One save covers every item on the page, and it records today's date on each decision you changed.

## If something is not as you expect

- An item with no decision shows *Not decided*, and the **Checks** panel lists it, for example *No decision yet on "Market Access Programme": invest, hold or stop.* Choose a decision and save again.
- The page says *No projects or programmes in this portfolio yet.* Nothing names the portfolio. Add items first.
- If you hold something because of what has to change first, write that in the **Reason**, so the next review can see what to look for.
- The reason is limited to 400 characters.
