# Ask a colleague a question

This guide shows you how to leave a comment beside part of a project and
ask a person or a team to answer. The example asks the quality team about
the *Scope* of the cooperative's *Quality Check Rollout*.

Comments are kept apart from the record. Anyone who may read a record can
comment on it.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Projects** and select *Quality Check Rollout*.
2. Open the **Scope** step. The comment mark sits beside the step's
   heading, and beside each deliverable on the **Deliverables** step. Its
   tooltip reads *Comment on Scope*.
3. Select the mark. A box opens titled *Comments on Scope*. It says *No
   comments yet. Ask a question, or ask someone with @.*
4. Write your question in the text box: *Does the scope include the
   Sales Ledger depots?*
5. To ask someone, type `@` and choose a person or a team from the list
   that opens. For the example, choose *Quality Team*.
6. Press **Send**, or Ctrl+Enter.

Your comment appears in the box with **Reply** and **Resolve** under it,
and the mark now shows *1 open comment on Scope*.
