# Cartograph guide

Cartograph helps an organisation write down what it has decided to do and
why: its purpose, goals, objectives and outcomes, the indicators that
measure them, the gaps it sees, and the work that closes them. From a
project's answers it writes a project charter.

This guide is for the people who use Cartograph, the people who run it,
and the people who build on it. Pick the door that matches what you want
to do now. You do not need to read it in order.

## New here?

Start with [Charter your first project](tutorials/charter-your-first-project.md).
In one sitting you open a workspace, start a project and read the charter
it produces. You do not need to know any project management words first.

When you have done that, [About walk-first](explanation/about-walk-first.md)
says why Cartograph asks one question at a time, and [About purpose,
goals, objectives and outcomes](explanation/about-purpose-goals-objectives-and-outcomes.md)
says why it asks for them in that order.
