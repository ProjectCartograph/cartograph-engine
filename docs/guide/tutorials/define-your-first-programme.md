# Define your first programme

In this tutorial we start a programme in the example workspace, say what
it is for, tie it to an outcome and an indicator, read its charter, and
merge it.

We use the produce cooperative that ships with Cartograph. Its members
deliver produce to depots and are paid for it each season. We define a
programme that makes sure members know the season's prices before they
choose what to deliver.

You need the example workspace open in your browser, and you need to be
allowed to edit it. To start one on your own computer, see
[Deploying Cartograph](../../DEPLOYMENT.md). You do not need to know any
project management words first.

## Part 1: Start the programme

1. Open Cartograph. You see the question *What are you working on?* and,
   under it, a row of what the workspace holds, including *2 Programmes*.

2. In the rail on the left, select **Programmes**. You see the list
   *Programmes* with two rows: *Market Access Programme* and *Quality
   Improvement Programme*.

3. Select **Programme**, the button with a plus beside it at the top
   right. You see *New programme*, with a box for *Name* and a box for
   *Lead team*.

4. In *Name*, type `Member Clarity Programme`.

5. Open *Lead team* and choose `Member Services Team`. The **Start**
   button, which was grey, turns dark.

6. Select **Start**. You see the page *Aim*. Above it is a row of eight
   steps: Aim, Problems, Alignment, Theory of change, Risks, Components,
   Stakeholders and Governance. Near the top of the screen, a box named
   *Changes to Member Clarity Programme* says *1 change*. Your work is
   kept there until we merge it.

## Part 2: Say what it is for

1. Under *Aim*, in the box *Intended change*, type `Every member knows the
   season's prices before they choose what to deliver.` You see *Saved in
   your draft* near the top right, and the *Aim* step in the row turns
   green.

2. In the box *Benefit*, type `Members can plan what to grow and when to
   deliver.` You see *Saved in your draft* again.

## Part 3: Tie it to an outcome and an indicator

1. In the row of steps, select **Alignment**. You see *Outcomes* and
   *Indicators*, each with a line that reads *Suggested from what you
   wrote*.

2. Under *Outcomes*, select the suggestion *Season results reach members
   on time*. A tick appears on the suggestion, and the same outcome is
   ticked in the list below it.

3. Under *Indicators*, select the suggestion *Member season earnings*.
   It gets a tick too. Further down, a box named *Checks* says *Nothing outstanding on this
   step*, and the *Alignment* step in the row turns green.

## Part 4: Read the charter

1. At the top right of the page, select **Charter**. You see *Programme
   charter* above the title *Member Clarity Programme*.

2. Read the first section, *At a glance*. It opens with the sentence you
   wrote as the intended change. Under it, *Lead team* says *Member
   Services Team* and *Goals* says *Season results reach members on
   time*. In the box at the top right of the charter, *Version* says
   *Working draft*.

## Part 5: Merge it

1. At the top right of the screen, select **Merge**. You see a box
   headed *Merge 1 record?* It lists three suggestions: the programme has
   no components, no theory of change and no problem yet.

2. In *Why merge before these are finished?*, type `First draft of the
   programme`.

3. Select **Merge anyway**. You see *Merged into the workspace.* at the
   top of the screen, and the charter now says *Version 1*.

4. In the rail, select **Programmes**. The list now has three
   programmes, and *Member Clarity Programme* sits under the heading
   *Run a dependable cooperative*.

We have defined a programme and read its charter. The three suggestions
we merged past are the rest of a programme: the problems it answers, how
it expects the change to happen, and the work inside it.
